# T010 — Final Local GPU Evaluation and Research Closure

## 1. Executive conclusion

**Engineering classification — GPU_VALUABLE_FOR_COMPACT_PROOF_STREAMS.** At n=1024, V9-GPU reduces median warm public replay from 10.505 to 7.606 seconds, a 1.381× speedup and 27.6% reduction. V6-CPU remains faster at 5.537 seconds. V9-GPU is 37.4% slower than V6 in replay alone, with a 63.513 MB transcript instead of V6's 150.578 MB.

**Publication recommendation — RETAIN_AS_VOTING_PROTOCOL_EXTENSION.** This is a useful optional backend for large compact-format verification and a measured format-selection tradeoff. It does not overturn T009's incremental-novelty assessment or justify an independent systems paper.

At n=64 the adaptive backend dispatches no work to the GPU. At n=256 V9-GPU is slightly slower than V9-CPU. At n=1024 GPU acceleration expands V9's useful bandwidth range. Under the stated analytical download model its crossing with V6 moves from approximately 140 to 337 Mbit/s. The conclusion is scoped to this measured implementation and host.

## 9. Remaining security and novelty limitations

Direct GPU verification assumes correct device arithmetic, driver behavior and host memory integrity. GPU inputs are public transcript points, responses, challenges and derived coefficients. Trustee secrets and witnesses remain in CPU production. Independent CPU replay checked every measured transcript. This experimental check does not constitute certified outsourcing.

O1 remains a commit–reveal realization of synchronous broadcast in the observable/programmed ROM. O2 is optional explicit slot-ciphertext binding in the Lin seed. O3 is formal multi-session batching within CGY's SUC composition. This performance task does not discharge these obligations or modify the frozen manuscript/security statements.

Use T009's corrected aggregate analysis. Fresh independent coefficients in [0,2^128) give at most 2^-128 false acceptance per nonzero prime-group aggregate, or B·2^-128 for B checks plus PRG/entropy advantage. This is not 1/q. Hash-derived Lin coefficients permit statement grinding, contributing 2Q1·2^-128. The replay bound is 2Q1·2^-128 + 2Q3·2^-250 + Qc·2^-250 + B·2^-128, plus PRG, signature and commitment-collision terms. At Q up to 2^64 the dominant term is about 2^-63. Fresh outer coefficients do not remove inner grinding.

Novelty judgments inherit T009's documented audit and its source-access limitations. One laptop, three repetitions and an analytical download model support a bounded engineering result. They do not establish physical WAN performance, independent trustee isolation or universal GPU benefit.
